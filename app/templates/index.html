About KisanProcure

KisanProcure is a digital procurement platform designed to bring transparency and efficiency to agricultural procurement. Our mission is to ensure farmers receive fair prices while minimizing wait times through smart slot booking.

MSP Guidelines

Minimum Support Price ensures fair compensation for farmers

The Government of India announces Minimum Support Prices (MSP) for various agricultural commodities before each sowing season. KisanProcure procurement centres operate under the MSP framework to ensure farmers receive guaranteed fair prices for their produce.
